Frequently asked questions

Who can buy these covers?

Individuals and families can buy medical, accident, education and investment products. Schools and employers can buy corporate medical, GPA/WIBA and pension schemes.

How much will cover cost?

Premiums depend on the product, the benefits you choose and the number of people covered. Contact us and we will prepare a quote for you.

What is the difference between GPA and WIBA?

GPA (Group Personal Accident) covers staff against accidental injury or death. WIBA (Work Injury Benefits Act) cover compensates employees for injury or illness arising from their work, and employers are required by law to provide it.

How do I make a claim?

Contact us as soon as possible after an incident. We guide you on the documents needed and help you submit the claim.

Are money-market returns guaranteed?

No. Returns vary with market conditions, so past performance does not guarantee future results. We can explain how the fund works before you invest.
